Habitats/03 movement

Learning to move

A plan is only as good as the planner's idea of its own body. Here each creature moves by pressing one of four buttons, wired to the four directions in a scrambled order. Walls block, and ice slides a creature one extra cell. Every 6,000 steps the wiring is turned a quarter turn, as if the body had changed. One planner is born knowing the wiring and the ground, and never questions either. The other learns movement from nothing: it tries each button once, then keeps a running count of which way each button moved it and what each kind of ground did, with old counts fading so that recent experience wins. Until the first rewiring, marked by a dashed line on the chart, the two should be hard to tell apart. The line under each table shows what the creature believes its buttons do, against what they really do.
