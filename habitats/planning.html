Habitats/02 planning

Planning a route

Both creatures here remember seasons in the same way. They differ in how they get to food. This world has walls, and stepping on any food eats it, wanted or not. The greedy creature picks the food it likes best and presses whichever button brings it closer, so it gets stuck behind walls and blunders through poison that lies in the way. The planner imagines its button presses before making them. It searches for a route, treats food it believes is bad as if it were a wall, and weighs each meal against the length of the walk to it. It plans again every few steps, and at once if a move does not land where it expected. The planner is told how movement works and only has to learn what food is worth. The dashed line shows where each creature is heading: the greedy one's points straight at its target, the planner's bends.
