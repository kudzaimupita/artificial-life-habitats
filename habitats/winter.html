Habitats/04 winter

Planning for winter

Every year of 2,400 steps ends in a winter of 800 steps in which nothing grows, and what food is left rots away. Neither creature can see a calendar. Both can feel the temperature, which falls towards midwinter and rises again. The unprepared planner ignores it and spends the winter searching an empty world at full cost. The winter planner learns two things from experience: how much food it usually sees at each temperature, and which temperature tends to follow 200 steps later. Together they make a forecast. When famine is forecast it walks further for a meal, and when famine has arrived and nothing in sight is worth eating it hibernates, which costs under a third of the energy of staying awake. Winters are the shaded bands on the chart. The first one catches both creatures equally; the experiment is about the ones after.
