Habitats/01 seasons

Seasons and memory

Two creatures live alone in identical worlds and must eat to stay alive. Every food has a colour and a shape, and what each is worth changes with the season, every 1,200 steps: food that fed a creature last season may cost it energy now. The simple creature keeps one set of beliefs and nudges them after every meal, so each change of season finds it eating poison until its beliefs come round. The memory creature treats a run of surprising meals as a sign that the world has changed. It sets those meals aside instead of overwriting what it knew, looks for an older memory that explains them, and switches to it or starts a new one. Now and then it sleeps, replaying recent meals and merging memories that turned out to be duplicates. The ticks under the chart are season changes. The experiment asks whether remembering old seasons beats relearning them every time.
