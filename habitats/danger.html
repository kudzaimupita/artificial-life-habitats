Habitats/05 danger

One-trial danger memory

In this world one food each season is nearly lethal: eating it costs 80 of a possible 100 energy. It is picked to look good by the usual rule, in which a food is worth its colour's value plus its shape's value, so it is a true exception. Both creatures plan routes and remember seasons. The gradual learner treats the deadly meal like any other surprise and spreads the blame over that food's colour and its shape, which puts it off harmless foods that only share one of them. The other creature has a danger memory. After a single near-fatal meal it remembers that exact food, refuses to step on it in any season, and leaves the rest of its beliefs alone. It drops the fear only if it ends up eating that food and comes to no harm. The deadly food, marked with a cross, changes with the season, so each creature has to meet each one at least once.
