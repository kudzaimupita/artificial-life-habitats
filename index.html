Habitats

Artificial life habitats

Small worlds in which a creature has to eat to stay alive while the rules keep changing. Each page puts two creatures in identical copies of one world. They differ in a single ability, so whatever separates them on the chart is down to that ability. Everything runs in the page from a seed you can set, and the same code runs headless to measure the difference over many worlds.

The rules every habitat shares

The world is a 24 by 24 grid holding 22 pieces of food. A food has one of three colours and one of two shapes, and is worth its colour's value plus its shape's value. Both values change with the season, so the same food can feed a creature in one season and poison it in the next. Food rots and regrows elsewhere.

A creature sees seven cells in each direction, spends a little energy on every step, and dies when it runs out. It then comes back somewhere else with its memories intact, so a run counts how often a mind fails rather than how long one body lasts.

Measuring it

One world proves little. The harness runs a habitat on several seeds without drawing anything and prints the comparison as a table. The same seed gives the same result in the page and on the command line. 

node harness/run.mjs --habitat seasons --seeds 8
